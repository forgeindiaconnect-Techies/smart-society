The society admin must provide accurate society name, address, block and flat details, authorized contact person, email, phone number, plan selection, and setup information. The registering user confirms they are authorized to create the SmartApartment workspace.

Flat and resident data

Admins are responsible for the accuracy of block names, flat numbers, resident mapping, ownership status, tenant details, parking information, occupancy status, billing rules, and role permissions. Incorrect setup may affect billing, visitor approvals, complaints, amenities, reports, and resident communication.

  • Verify imported data before inviting residents.
  • Do not upload unnecessary sensitive data during onboarding.
  • Keep backups of original import sheets until setup is reviewed and accepted.

  • Do not share OTPs, UPI PINs, card CVV, banking passwords, or wallet credentials with any staff member.

  • Duplicate or wrong payments may be adjusted or refunded according to society policy, gateway rules, and bank settlement status.

Receipts, Payment Proof, and Reconciliation

When SmartApartment requires residents to upload screenshots or receipts, the user must upload clear and genuine proof that matches the bill, amount, flat number, payment date, and transaction reference. False, edited, reused, or unrelated screenshots may lead to rejection or account restriction.

Admin verification

Admins must verify uploaded proof before marking a bill paid. Receipt generation should reflect the correct resident, flat, month, amount, payment mode, transaction reference, and approval status. Incorrect receipts should be corrected through an auditable adjustment or admin note.

  • Residents should keep transaction proof until the society confirms payment posting.
  • Admins and super admins may review payment proof for reconciliation, audit, and dispute resolution.
  • Manual mark-paid actions must be used carefully and only by authorized admins.
