Maintenance Billing Terms

Scope of billing

Society admins are responsible for configuring billing cycles, due dates, maintenance amounts, late fees, taxes, utility recovery, sinking fund, parking charges, common-area charges, and flat-wise charge rules. The platform provides the tools to create and publish bills, but the society remains responsible for the commercial accuracy of every bill.

Charge rules should be approved by the association, committee, builder, or authorized management body before they are entered into SmartApartment. If the society uses different rates by block, flat size, occupancy, parking slot, or resident type, those rules must be configured before the billing cycle is generated.

Review, correction, and publishing

Generated bills should be reviewed before publishing to residents. Corrections must be issued through adjustments, credit notes, waiver entries, or revised invoices so that the audit trail remains clear. Admins should not delete billing history merely to hide an incorrect bill or payment follow-up.

Residents must pay dues according to the society-approved schedule and retain receipts for reference. If a resident disputes a charge, the resident should raise the issue before the due date where possible, and the admin should record the final decision inside the platform or in an approved society communication channel.

Resident responsibilities

  • Verify flat number, bill month, amount, due date, and payment purpose before paying.
  • Report duplicate bills, incorrect arrears, wrong flat mapping, or missing receipts promptly.
  • Keep payment proof until the society confirms settlement and ledger update.
  • Avoid sharing OTPs, card details, banking passwords, or wallet credentials with any staff member.

Online Payment Terms

Payment processing

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways, banks, wallet providers, UPI networks, or other payment partners. Gateway fees, settlement timelines, bank holidays, failed transactions, reversals, chargebacks, and refunds follow the applicable provider rules.

A payment is treated as successful only after confirmation is received from the payment provider and recorded in SmartApartment. Screenshots, pending bank debits, or user-side notifications may help in support review, but they do not by themselves complete ledger posting.

Failed, duplicate, and refunded payments

If a payment fails but money is debited, the resident should wait for provider reconciliation or contact the payment provider through the approved support channel. Duplicate payments may be adjusted against future dues or refunded according to society policy, gateway rules, and bank settlement status.

SmartApartment may display transaction status, references, and receipts based on provider data. The platform is not responsible for incorrect account numbers, wrong bill selection, bank downtime, user network failure, or delays caused by external providers.

Security and fraud prevention

  • Residents should not share payment credentials, OTPs, card details, UPI PINs, or banking passwords with society staff or platform support.
  • Admins should reconcile collections with bank statements before treating monthly collection reports as final.
  • Suspicious payment links, refund requests, or unofficial collection accounts should be reported immediately.
  • Receipts may be reissued only when the transaction reference and ledger entry can be matched.

Expense Approval Terms

Expense submission

Expenses must include vendor name, amount, category, invoice date, proof document, payment mode, tax details where applicable, and approval status. Expenses should be entered under the correct category so that monthly reports and audit summaries remain meaningful.

Recurring expenses such as security salary, housekeeping, lift maintenance, water supply, electricity, internet, pest control, and repairs should be recorded consistently. One-time emergency expenses should include a short reason and supporting proof whenever available.

Approval workflow

Committee members or authorized admins are responsible for approving, rejecting, or requesting clarification for submitted expenses. Approvers should check the invoice, work completion, vendor identity, amount, and budget impact before approval.

SmartApartment records approval history for transparency and audit reference. Users must not approve their own reimbursement unless society policy expressly permits it. Any conflict of interest should be disclosed to the committee before approval.

Audit and retention

  • Proof documents should be legible and related to the expense being claimed.
  • Rejected expenses should include a reason so the submitter can correct or close the entry.
  • Approved expenses should not be edited after payment without creating an adjustment note.
  • Exported expense reports must be shared only with authorized society users, auditors, or committee members.

Reports and Analytics Terms

Report sources

Reports are generated from data entered by society admins, residents, security staff, maintenance teams, payment systems, and configured integrations. Report accuracy depends on correct user entries, timely updates, and successful synchronization with connected services.

Analytics should be used for operational decisions, audit support, billing follow-up, visitor review, complaint tracking, budget review, occupancy analysis, and committee reporting. They should not be treated as legal, tax, accounting, or compliance advice without review by the appropriate professional.

Use and sharing

Exported reports may contain sensitive resident, visitor, payment, complaint, or financial data and must be shared only with authorized users. The society is responsible for deciding who may download, email, print, or forward reports.

Admins should verify filters, date ranges, tenant selection, and export format before using reports for official decisions. Where reports are used in committee meetings, the report date and generation time should be recorded to avoid confusion with later updates.

Data protection

  • Do not publish resident contact lists, visitor logs, or payment reports in public groups.
  • Remove unnecessary personal data before sharing summaries outside the management committee.
  • Report suspected unauthorized exports or unusual download activity to the platform owner.
  • Keep downloaded reports in secured drives with access limited to authorized members.
