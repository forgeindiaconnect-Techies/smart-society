Resident Onboarding Terms

Resident verification

Residents may be invited by the society admin or approved after verification. The admin must confirm flat ownership, tenancy, occupancy period, and contact details before granting access to resident tools.

Resident access should be given only to the correct owner, tenant, family member, or authorized occupant. Temporary users, staff, and guests should not be added as permanent residents unless society policy permits it.

Profile responsibilities

Residents are responsible for keeping profile, family member, vehicle, pet, emergency contact, and communication details accurate inside the platform. Incorrect profile details may affect visitor approvals, emergency communication, billing reminders, and complaint follow-up.

Access may be revoked when a resident vacates, ownership changes, tenancy ends, or the admin detects misuse. Historical payment, complaint, or visitor records may be retained according to society policy and audit needs.

Acceptable use

  • Do not share resident login credentials with other flats or outside users.
  • Use announcements, complaints, and visitor approvals only for genuine society needs.
  • Report incorrect flat mapping or unauthorized access immediately.
  • Respect privacy of other residents when using community features.

Visitor Approval Terms

Entry creation

Visitor entries must be created with correct visitor name, purpose, expected time, flat number, vehicle information where required, and approval status. Residents should approve only known visitors or expected deliveries.

Security staff may verify visitor identity at the gate and record check-in/check-out times. Residents are responsible for approving known visitors, while security staff are responsible for following gate protocol and recording movement accurately.

Gate rules and exceptions

Emergency access, delivery access, vendor access, cab entry, domestic staff access, and service staff access may follow society-specific rules configured by the admin. Local security instructions may override app convenience features during emergencies.

Visitor logs may contain personal data and should be used only for security, audit, incident review, and society operations. Users must not export, publish, or misuse visitor contact details.

Security checklist

  • Confirm visitor identity and destination before check-in.
  • Record check-out when visitors leave the premises.
  • Escalate suspicious, rejected, or emergency entries to the authorized admin.
  • Do not bypass society rules for convenience or personal requests.

Complaint Assignment Terms

Complaint creation

Complaints must include clear issue details, category, priority, location, flat number where relevant, and supporting images when required. Residents should avoid duplicate complaints for the same issue unless the earlier complaint has not been addressed within the agreed response time.

Emergency issues such as fire, gas leak, lift entrapment, flooding, or electrical hazard should also be reported through the society's emergency process, not only through the app.

Assignment and closure

Admins may assign complaints to maintenance teams, vendors, facility managers, or committee members. Assigned users must update progress honestly, add work notes when required, and close only after resolution or documented rejection.

Residents may reopen or escalate complaints if the issue remains unresolved. Admins should record final resolution details, cost implications, vendor involvement, and any resident confirmation where society policy requires it.

Misuse controls

  • Abusive content, harassment, false emergency reporting, or personal attacks may lead to account restriction.
  • Photos should relate to the issue and must not violate another resident's privacy.
  • Maintenance staff should not mark tasks complete without performing the assigned work.
  • Repeated duplicate complaints may be merged for operational clarity.

Amenity Booking Terms

Booking eligibility

Amenity bookings depend on availability, society rules, time slots, payment requirements, resident eligibility, and admin approval settings. Amenities may include clubhouse, gym, guest rooms, parking, party hall, sports facilities, community rooms, and shared equipment.

The platform may show available slots based on configured rules, but the society may still reject or reschedule a booking due to maintenance, emergency use, unpaid dues, overbooking, rule violations, or committee decision.

Usage responsibilities

Residents must follow usage rules for cleanliness, guest count, noise limits, time limits, prohibited items, safety requirements, and handover condition. The booking resident is responsible for guests and any damage caused during the booked slot.

Cancellation, refund, security deposit, damage charges, guest limits, and late-use penalties may vary by society policy. Residents should read amenity-specific rules before confirming a paid or approval-based booking.

Operational rules

  • Do not transfer bookings to another flat without admin approval.
  • Arrive and vacate within the approved time slot.
  • Report damaged equipment or unsafe conditions before use.
  • Follow staff instructions during maintenance, emergency, or crowd-control situations.
