Ticket triage details

Use these checks before moving a complaint into your active work orders.

Water leakage casesConfirm source isolation, the affected flat, lower-floor impact, resident access and emergency contact before accepting.
Electrical casesCheck power isolation, public-area safety, access route and whether an approved vendor is required.
Resident availabilityRecord visit window, preferred contact number and security-entry instruction in the work log.
Parts readinessList consumables and vendor parts before starting, so the admin can approve urgent purchases quickly.

Claim and handover checklist

Every accepted ticket should have these minimum details.

  1. Review priority, reported time and SLA deadline.
  2. Confirm the exact flat, access window and contact person.
  3. Check whether tools, spare parts or a vendor are required.
  4. Add a work-start note after you claim the ticket.
  5. Record completion photos and resident confirmation before closure.
