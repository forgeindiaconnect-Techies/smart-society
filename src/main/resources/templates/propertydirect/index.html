Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for a property? +

Simply select your desired location, choose between Buy or Rent options, and enter your locality. You can use advanced filters to narrow down your search results based on price, BHK, and amenities.

Are all properties verified on the platform? +

Yes! Every single property listed goes through our rigorous 3-step verification process to ensure complete transparency and authenticity.

Is there really zero brokerage involved? +

Absolutely. We connect buyers and tenants directly with property owners, completely eliminating any middleman or brokerage fees from the transaction.

How do I list my property? +

Click on the 'Post Property' button on the top navigation bar. Fill in your property details, upload quality photos, and your listing will be live instantly after a quick verification.
