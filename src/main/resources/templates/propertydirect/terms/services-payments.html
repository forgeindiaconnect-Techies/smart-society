Home Services Terms

Service partner model

Home services are fulfilled by service partners, listed vendors, or independent professionals. Service availability, pricing, time slots, warranties, and rescheduling depend on location, vendor confirmation, society access rules, and customer readiness.

PropertyDirect may help with discovery, booking, payment initiation, and support coordination, but the actual service quality and completion are handled by the assigned partner unless expressly stated otherwise.

  • Confirm final scope, price, arrival time, materials, and warranty before work begins.
  • Keep valuables, documents, keys, and fragile items secured during service visits.
  • Report service issues with photos, booking details, and partner name where possible.

Painting Terms

Quotation basis

Painting quotes depend on wall condition, paint type, room size, labor, surface preparation, putty, primer, repair needs, color selection, scaffolding, and furniture movement. Final cost may change after inspection.

Customers should confirm whether the quote includes wall repairs, damp treatment, ceiling painting, doors, grills, windows, texture work, cleanup, and paint brand. Color variation may occur due to lighting, wall texture, and screen display differences.

  • Approve shade codes and finish type before work starts.
  • Protect or move furniture, curtains, electronics, and personal items.
  • Inspect the work before final handover and record touch-up requests promptly.

Home Cleaning Terms

Scope and exclusions

Cleaning scope should be confirmed before booking. Deep cleaning, move-in cleaning, bathroom cleaning, kitchen cleaning, balcony cleaning, appliance cleaning, and recurring cleaning may have different inclusions and exclusions.

Cleaning results depend on property condition, stain age, grease buildup, water quality, ventilation, material sensitivity, and access to electricity and water. Permanent damage, old stains, mold inside walls, pest infestation, and renovation debris may require separate services.

  • Remove personal items and fragile objects from work areas.
  • Confirm whether cabinets, appliances, fans, windows, and balconies are included.
  • Inspect the service before the team leaves the premises.

Apartment Moving Terms

Estimate factors

Moving service estimates depend on item count, distance, floor, lift access, packing material, vehicle type, society timing restrictions, dismantling needs, and manpower. Final charges may change if additional items or access constraints are found.

Customers should disclose fragile items, heavy appliances, plants, valuables, documents, pets, restricted items, and society move-in/move-out permissions before booking. Insurance, if available, must be understood before packing begins.

  • Keep jewelry, cash, documents, and irreplaceable items with yourself.
  • Get gate pass and lift permissions before the moving slot.
  • Check inventory and visible damage at pickup and delivery.

Plumbing and Carpentry Terms

Inspection and approval

Repair charges may include visit fees, labor, spare parts, emergency service fees, and repeat visits. Customers should approve work scope, estimated cost, and part replacement before repair begins.

Hidden leakage, wall damage, old fixtures, concealed pipes, termite damage, or non-standard fittings may require additional work. The vendor may refuse unsafe, illegal, or structurally risky work.

  • Ask for replaced part details and warranty where available.
  • Do not request temporary fixes that create safety risks for other residents.
  • Report post-service leakage or fitting issues within the stated support window.

Electrician Terms

Safety requirements

Electrical work must follow basic safety practices and building rules. Users should not request unsafe wiring, overload changes, meter tampering, bypass connections, or work that violates society, utility, or statutory requirements.

Electrical estimates may change after inspection of wiring condition, load capacity, circuit breaker condition, earthing, appliance rating, and concealed wiring. Power shutdown permission may be required for some tasks.

  • Turn off sensitive appliances before repair where instructed.
  • Use compatible, rated parts for switches, wiring, and breakers.
  • Stop work immediately if burning smell, sparks, or overheating occurs.

Sofa Cleaning Terms

Fabric limitations

Cleaning outcome depends on fabric type, stain age, chemical compatibility, previous damage, color fastness, and usage. Some stains, odor, discoloration, shrinkage, or fabric wear may not be fully removable.

Customers should disclose delicate fabric, leather, suede, pet stains, food stains, ink, previous chemical use, and existing tears before service. Patch testing may be recommended for sensitive materials.

  • Allow proper drying time after cleaning.
  • Keep children and pets away until the sofa is dry.
  • Report concerns immediately after inspection.

Kitchen Cleaning Terms

Included areas

Kitchen cleaning scope may include chimney, tiles, slabs, cabinets, sink, exhaust, floor, stove area, and appliances only when selected. Users must secure fragile items, food, medicines, and personal belongings before service.

Heavy grease, rust, old stains, pest residue, blocked drains, appliance interiors, and chimney dismantling may need separate charges or specialist service. Water and electricity must be available during the booking slot.

  • Confirm whether inside cabinets and appliances are included.
  • Remove utensils and food items from cleaning zones.
  • Inspect tiles, chimney area, sink, and slab before completion.

Bathroom Cleaning Terms

Cleaning limits

Bathroom cleaning may not remove permanent stains, tile damage, rust, old grout discoloration, hard-water scaling, or broken sealant. Ventilation, water access, and safe drainage must be available.

Customers should disclose loose fittings, broken glass, slippery flooring, blocked drains, or electrical points near wet areas before service. Some chemical use may not be suitable for natural stone or delicate fixtures.

  • Remove personal toiletries and towels before service.
  • Keep the area ventilated after chemical cleaning.
  • Report fixture damage before the team starts work.

Bill Payment Terms

Payment verification

Bill payment features help users initiate rent, maintenance, and utility transactions through supported payment workflows. Users must verify amount, recipient, consumer number, due date, payment purpose, and charges before submitting.

PropertyDirect may display transaction references and payment status based on provider responses. A payment is considered complete only after provider confirmation and successful posting to the relevant account or recipient.

  • Do not share OTPs, UPI PINs, card details, or banking passwords.
  • Keep receipts until the recipient confirms payment.
  • Report duplicate or failed payments with transaction reference details.

Rent Payment Terms

Rent transfer terms

Rent payments may be processed by payment providers. Failed payments, refunds, bank delays, payment limits, convenience fees, and charges follow provider rules.

Users should verify the landlord or owner account, amount, rental month, and payment notes before paying. PropertyDirect is not responsible for disputes caused by wrong recipient details entered by the user.

  • Use official receipts or transaction references for rent records.
  • Do not pay deposits or token amounts to unverified accounts.
  • Coordinate refund or adjustment requests with the owner and payment provider.

Maintenance Payment Terms

Society dues

Maintenance payment amounts are set by the owner, society, association, or facility manager. Users should verify bill amount, due date, penalty, flat number, and billing month before payment.

Payment posting may depend on society confirmation, provider settlement, or platform reconciliation. Wrong flat mapping or incorrect amount should be reported before making another payment.

  • Confirm whether parking, water, power backup, and common charges are included.
  • Retain receipts until the ledger is updated.
  • Raise disputes through the society or owner before the due date when possible.

Utility Bill Terms

Provider dependency

Utility bill payment depends on supported providers and billing details entered by the user. PropertyDirect is not responsible for wrong consumer numbers, provider outages, late provider updates, disconnection actions, or billing disputes with utility companies.

Users should verify biller name, consumer id, amount, due date, surcharge, and previous balance before submitting. Some providers may take additional time to update paid status.

  • Pay before the due date to avoid provider late fees or disconnection.
  • Check provider receipts for electricity, water, gas, or internet bills.
  • Contact the provider directly for meter disputes or incorrect bill generation.
