Post Property Policy

Owner Listing Terms

Posting authority

The person posting a property confirms that they are the owner or have authorization from the owner to list the apartment. Unauthorized listings, duplicate postings, fake owner claims, impersonation, misleading broker-style listings, or bait listings may be removed.

Platform review

PropertyDirect may ask for supporting details to confirm ownership or listing permission. Review may include phone verification, document checks, property address confirmation, previous listing checks, or complaint history review.

  • Post only properties that are genuinely available for rent, sale, or premium enquiry.
  • Do not list another person's property without written permission.
  • Update or remove listings immediately when they are no longer available.

Property Detail Terms

Accuracy of details

Posters must provide accurate apartment type, block, floor, area, furnishing, parking, amenities, possession date, facing, age of property, society name, locality, and access conditions. Misleading details can cause listing suspension.

Required disclosures

Material facts such as pending litigation, major repairs, seepage, water issues, power backup limitations, association restrictions, pet rules, tenant restrictions, or redevelopment plans should be disclosed to interested users.

  • Do not inflate carpet area, amenities, rent, deposit refund promise, or furnishing level.
  • Confirm whether maintenance, parking, club fees, and utility deposits are included.
  • Keep visit availability and possession dates current.

Photos and Media Terms

Media ownership

Uploaded images should represent the actual apartment and must not include unrelated stock photos, offensive content, watermarked competitor images, misleading edits, or private information of current residents.

Moderation and usage

PropertyDirect may resize, crop, compress, watermark, reorder, or moderate media for layout, safety, and listing quality. By uploading photos, the poster confirms they have permission to use them for the listing.

  • Use recent photos of rooms, kitchen, bathroom, balcony, building, and amenities.
  • Remove personal documents, family photos, number plates, and sensitive items from images.
  • Do not use AI-generated or sample images as actual property photos.

Pricing and Availability Terms

Price updates

Rent, sale price, deposit, maintenance, token amount, brokerage status, move-in charges, and availability must be kept up to date. Owners are responsible for updating the listing when the apartment is booked, rented, sold, reserved, or temporarily unavailable.

Negotiation and agreements

Negotiations, payment terms, refund terms, agreement conditions, and handover obligations are between the owner and customer. PropertyDirect may assist with workflow, but it does not guarantee that a negotiation will close.

  • Do not ask users to pay token amounts before basic verification and visit coordination.
  • Clearly state non-refundable fees before accepting payment.
  • Mark the listing unavailable when final agreement is signed.

Verification and Contact Terms

Contact sharing

Owner contact details may be shared with interested users according to platform workflow. Posters must not misuse customer data received through PropertyDirect or contact users for unrelated marketing.

Misuse and enforcement

Spam, harassment, repeated unsolicited calls, pressure tactics, fake urgency, off-platform payment demands, or asking users to pay outside agreed terms may lead to restriction. PropertyDirect may hide listings or block accounts after complaints or verification failure.

  • Use customer contact details only for the specific property enquiry.
  • Keep call, visit, and negotiation communication professional.
  • Report suspicious customers, fake documents, or abusive behavior through support.
