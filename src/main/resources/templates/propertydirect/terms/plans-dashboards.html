Assisted Plan Terms

Plan scope

Assisted plans provide guidance, follow-up, shortlisting support, visit coordination, and matching help based on the selected package. They do not guarantee final property selection, owner acceptance, agreement execution, price negotiation outcome, loan approval, or move-in completion.

Activation and expiry

Plan benefits may begin only after payment confirmation and requirement collection. Benefits may be limited by city, listing availability, partner availability, user responsiveness, and plan validity period.

  • Read plan inclusions, exclusions, refund terms, and expiry before purchase.
  • Keep requirements updated when budget, city, or move-in timeline changes.
  • Use assisted support only for genuine property search needs.

Property Support Terms

Support activities

Support may include shortlist help, visit coordination, call reminders, requirement collection, owner follow-up, service suggestions, and dashboard guidance. Users must verify property details personally before decisions.

User cooperation

Support quality depends on user responsiveness, clear requirements, realistic budget, available inventory, and owner cooperation. Missed calls, delayed replies, repeated requirement changes, or unavailable visit slots may affect service timelines.

  • Share accurate city, budget, family size, furnishing needs, and move-in date.
  • Attend confirmed visits or cancel in advance.
  • Confirm all final terms directly with the owner before payment or agreement.

Matching Alert Terms

Alert logic

Matching alerts are based on city, locality, budget, property type, BHK, furnishing, amenities, availability, and selected filters. Alerts may include approximate matches when exact listings are unavailable.

Accuracy and timing

Alerts depend on listing data supplied by owners and admins. Availability, price, and owner response may change before the user contacts the listing. PropertyDirect may pause, refine, or stop alerts when user preferences are inactive or invalid.

  • Review every alert before relying on it for travel or payment decisions.
  • Update preferences to reduce irrelevant matches.
  • Report stale, duplicate, or misleading alerts through support.

Owner Contact Assistance Terms

Contact use

Owner contact information is shared for genuine property discussions only. Users must not spam, harass, scrape, resell, publish, or misuse contact information obtained through PropertyDirect.

Communication conduct

Users and owners must communicate respectfully and only about the relevant property enquiry. PropertyDirect may restrict accounts that repeatedly call at unreasonable times, pressure users, share abusive messages, or misuse unlocked contacts.

  • Do not forward owner numbers to brokers or marketing lists.
  • Confirm visit timing before arriving at the property.
  • Report fake contacts, wrong numbers, or harassment to platform support.

Super Admin Dashboard Terms

Platform authority

Super Admin access is for platform-level management, including users, plans, listings, payments, services, analytics, support queues, and system configuration. Credentials must be protected and not shared.

Operational accountability

Super Admin actions may affect multiple users and listings. Changes to plans, payments, listing visibility, support status, and account access should be made only by authorized platform operators and should remain auditable.

  • Use strong passwords and revoke access when staff roles change.
  • Do not export customer or owner data without a legitimate business purpose.
  • Review suspicious activity, payment issues, and complaint patterns promptly.
Page 6

Admin Dashboard Terms

Admin responsibilities

Admins manage property listings, owner requests, verification workflow, lead handling, visit schedules, payments, and local operations. Admin actions should be accurate, timely, and auditable.

Listing and lead handling

Admins must not manipulate listings, fabricate leads, hide complaints, or mark properties verified without required checks. Lead data should be used only for the property enquiry and not for unrelated marketing.

  • Keep listing review notes clear and professional.
  • Update lead status after calls, visits, and owner responses.
  • Escalate fraud, fake owner claims, and payment complaints quickly.
Page 7

Customer Dashboard Terms

Customer tools

Customers can shortlist apartments, request owner contact, track visits, manage rent/service/payment requests, view plans, and raise support tickets. Customer data must be accurate and used only for legitimate property needs.

Account and data use

Customers are responsible for keeping profile details, phone number, email, preferences, and payment information accurate. Misuse of dashboard features, fake enquiries, abusive support messages, or repeated no-show visits may lead to restrictions.

  • Use shortlist and contact tools only for properties you are genuinely considering.
  • Cancel visits you cannot attend.
  • Review payment details carefully before rent, service, or plan payments.
